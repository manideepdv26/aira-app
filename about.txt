Telangana[a] is a state in southern India on the Deccan Plateau bordering Maharashtra and Chhattisgarh to the north 
and Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka to the south. It is the eleventh largest and the twelfth most populated state of 
India.[13][14] According to the Rigveda's Aitareya Brahmana, the region corresponding to Telangana has been 
inhabited by the Asmakas and Andhras since at least the 8th and 9th century BCE, respectively, with the later 
Satavahana dynasty, who ruled over the entire Deccan Plateau, establishing trade relations as far as the Roman Empire.
